100,000-User Scalability

Unit Economics & Cost

Voice AI COGS is ~$0.032/min ($0.004 STT + $0.002 Flash LLM + $0.015 TTS + $0.009 SIP/WebRTC). At 150 mins/user/mo, gross margin exceeds 84% on $299–$899 SaaS tiers.

Security & Compliance
